# Intermediate B Book A — Book Notes

5th-level. Sits between Intermediate A and Higher A in the curriculum.
47 pages (cover + 46), landscape A4.

## What This Book Teaches

- 8-row Abacus Calc with 2-3 digit numbers
- Mental Arithmetic with shorter time limits (5 mins)
- Multiplication tables continued
- Some division intro

## Spine

- p1-12: Abacus Calc 8-row, 3-digit
- p13-18: Mental Arithmetic 4-5-row tables, 5-min limits
- p19-22: Multiplication ×8, ×9 + bigger numbers
- p23-29: Mixed Mental/Abacus
- p30-37: Division and mixed
- p38-45: Speed sprints
- p46: Final Assessment
