# Higher A Book A — Book Notes

6th-level (UCMAS terminology). 47 pages (cover + 46). Competition-prep tier.

Reference: UCMAS (`Higher-A Book-A (1-24).pdf` + `Higher-A Book-A (25-46).pdf`).

## What This Book Teaches

Mastery / competition prep:
- **3-digit and 4-digit** Abacus Calculation
- **Mental Arithmetic** with very high speed (5-8 min for 30 problems)
- **Multi-digit multiplication** (3-digit × 1-digit)
- **Division** (4-digit ÷ 1-digit)

## Spine

- p1-12: Multi-row Abacus Calc with 3-4 digit numbers
- p13-20: Mental Arithmetic 30-problem 5-min sprints
- p21-29: Multiplication (×N) including multi-digit
- p30-37: Division
- p38-45: Mixed assessment / revision
- p46: Final competition-style assessment

Built standalone.
