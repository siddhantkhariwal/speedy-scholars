# Intermediate A Book A — Book Notes

4th-level (UCMAS terminology). 47 pages (cover + 46), landscape A4.

Reference: UCMAS (`public/ucmas-books/INTERMEDIATE-A (BOOK-A) 1-24.pdf` +
`INTERMEDIATE A(BOOK-A) 25-46.pdf`).

## What This Book Teaches

Speed mastery of 2-digit calculations + multiplication tables. Builds on
Elementary B Book A. Three themes:

1. **Abacus Calculation** with 8-row tables of 2-digit numbers
2. **Mental Arithmetic** with 5-minute time limits — high-speed drilling
3. **Multiplication** tables × 4, × 5, × 6, × 7 in A/B/C/D group format
